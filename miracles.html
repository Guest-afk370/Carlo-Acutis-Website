Miracles from his Tomb

Grace and Wonder

The witness of Carlo Acutis continues through reports of healing and faith connected to his tomb and his prayers.

The Tomb in Assisi

From Life to Prayer

After Carlo’s death, many people were drawn to his life and his example of holiness. His body was placed in Assisi, where people began to pray at his tomb and ask for his intercession.

Reports of graces and healings linked to Carlo’s tomb became important signs for the Church. These experiences helped many people discover the importance of prayer, trust, and the presence of Christ in suffering.

Why People Pray at the Tomb

People visit the tomb of Carlo Acutis because they see in him a young Christian witness who loved the Eucharist and lived with a strong sense of friendship and prayer. His example encourages people to bring their needs to God through the Church.

Through stories of healing and answered prayers, Carlo’s tomb became a place of hope for many. The Church carefully studies reports of miracles and graces before recognizing them as a sign of a person’s holiness.

A Witness of Peace

Carlo’s life did not end with illness or death. His faith, his witness, and the reports of grace linked to his tomb continue to speak to believers. People remember him as a young saintly witness who helped them see the value of praying with Christ.
