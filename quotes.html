Quotes of Carlo Acutis

Words of Faith

Carlo Acutis spoke simply and clearly about faith, friendship, prayer, and the Eucharist.

Carlo Acutis
“The Eucharist is my highway to heaven.”

A simple phrase that describes his deep love for the Eucharist.

Life
“To be always united with Jesus is my life program.”

Carlo’s guiding path of faith and friendship with Christ.

Saintliness
“We don’t need superpowers to be saints, we just need to love.”

A reminder that holiness grows through love.

Motherhood
“Only in heaven will we see how much we owe to our mothers.”

A reflection on family love and gratitude.

Life
“Life is a gift, and we don’t have to waste it.”

A call to live each day with purpose.

Happiness
“Genuine happiness can only be found in God.”

A spiritual reflection on true joy.

Prayer
“Prayer is the best weapon we have.”

A strong statement about the power of prayer.

Faith
“Heaven is filled with people who were considered losers on earth.”

A reminder that God sees hidden worth.

Mission
“My ideal is to unite all people with Christ.”

A vision of evangelisation and friendship.

Suffering
“Suffering is a gift from God.”

A humble teaching about the meaning of suffering.

Saints
“Let’s be saints of the new millennium.”

A call to holiness in the modern age.

Courage
“Be brave! Be courageous! Don’t let fear stop you from loving.”

A message of courage, love, and trust.

Encounter
“Every encounter with God is a treasure.”

A reflection on the beauty of prayer and faith.

Love
“The more we love, the closer we are to God.”

An invitation to grow in charity.

Humility
“Humility is the foundation of all virtues.”

A call to live with a simple and holy heart.

Peace
“Do not let your heart be troubled.”

A prayerful message of peace and trust.
