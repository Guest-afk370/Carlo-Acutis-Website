A Life of Joy and Faith

St. Carlo Acutis was born in 1991 in London and grew up in Italy. He was known for his love of the Eucharist, his sense of friendship, and his ability to use technology for a greater purpose.

He created websites that shared stories of miracles and the Catholic faith. Through his work, he showed that digital tools can help people understand truth, beauty, and holiness.

Carlo’s project: [MIRACOLI EUCARISTICI - Mostra Internazionale Ideata e Realizzata da Carlo Acutis e Nicola Gori]

"The Eucharist is my highway to heaven."

His Mission

Carlo inspired others to see faith as a living story. He encouraged young people to pray, to serve, and to use their skills to lead others closer to Christ.

Why He Matters

His life reminds us that holiness can be found in everyday life. Carlo showed that young people can be joyful, thoughtful, and deeply committed to the Church.

Today, his example continues to encourage Catholics and students across the world.
